Security

How your data is protected

Shiftmates stores only what it needs to send your reminders. Your address is used for the reminders and nothing else.

Your email is verified
Nobody can sign you up but you.
Passwords are hashed with bcrypt
They are never stored in plain text.
Repeated wrong passwords lock the account
After five wrong tries it locks for 15 minutes.
You can stop the emails at any time
One page in your account turns both reminders off at once.
