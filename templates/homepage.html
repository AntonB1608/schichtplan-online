How it works

Three steps, then it runs

Set it up once. Nothing to open again until your roster changes.

  1. Save a shift

    A date, a start and an end time, and the kind of shift it is.

  2. One email in the evening

    At 18:00 by default, telling you what tomorrow looks like.

  3. One before you leave

    Anywhere from 30 minutes to 3 hours ahead. You pick the lead time.

Both run in your own timezone, and either one can be switched off on its own.

Why it exists

Built by someone who worked the shifts

I worked full-time at a public swimming pool. Like almost everywhere, the roster hung on the wall. When it changed, whoever was off that day found out late. Nobody’s fault, telling everyone one by one just takes too long. I overslept a shift myself.

Shiftmates does one thing: it gets your shifts to you, the evening before and again before you leave. It sells nothing, and your address is used for the reminders and nothing else.
