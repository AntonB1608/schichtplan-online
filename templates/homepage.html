How it works

  1. Save a shift

    A date with a start and an end time. Or mark the day as time off.

  2. The night before, an email

    By default at 22:00, telling you what tomorrow looks like.

  3. On the day, one more

    By default at 05:00, so you know before you leave the house.

Both times are yours to change, in your own timezone.

What arrives

Shiftmates 22:00

Reminder for tomorrow

Good evening Marc,

You work tomorrow from 06:00 to 14:30.

Light drizzle expected tomorrow. 7°C

Days off get their own email, so a missing message never means a missed shift.
